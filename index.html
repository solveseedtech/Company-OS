How can you deliver a production MVP in 5–7 days and full SaaS in 10–14 days? +

Are future customizations and updates really 100% included in maintenance? +
Yes, 100%. Unlike freelancers who vanish after delivering code, or agencies that charge expensive hourly rates for every small request, Solve Seeds acts as your permanent tech team. Every future workflow change, dashboard tweak, third-party API integration, and feature enhancement is included with zero hourly fees.
Do we own 100% of our code and intellectual property? +
Yes. You receive full Git repository transfers, server deployment scripts, and complete documentation from day one. There are zero licensing royalties, zero vendor lock-in, and zero hidden dependencies. You own every single line of code forever.
How do Indian payments (UPI 2.0 Autopay) and GST e-invoices work? +
We build native Indian payment rails directly into your platform. This includes recurring UPI 2.0 Autopay subscriptions, instant bank notifications, automated vendor payouts, and compliant B2B GST e-invoices with official IRN numbers and signed QR codes.
How is Solve Seeds lower cost than hiring in-house or an agency? +
Hiring an internal team requires recruiter fees, high salaries ($150k–$250k/year per engineer), and 3 to 6 months of onboarding. Traditional agencies charge high retainer overhead and punitive change orders. We price upfront based on your exact project specification—saving you over 60% while including lifetime maintenance.
How does the platform handle traffic scaling past 100,000 users? +
Our architecture is built for high concurrency from day one. We use database connection pooling, in-memory Redis caching, and fast cloud servers across Mumbai, Bengaluru, and Delhi. As your user base grows, your assigned engineering team proactively optimizes queries, caching, and server clusters.
