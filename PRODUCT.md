## Platform

## Stack

Pure Vanilla HTML5 / Modern CSS / Vanilla JS with zero build dependencies, pure native DOM manipulation, and responsive exposed hairline grid system.

## Users

Non-technical founders, seed/Series A startups, and enterprise engineering leaders who need custom B2B SaaS products or internal tools deployed in 21–35 days without unpredictable hourly agency billing, slow delivery, or paying to reinvent basic plumbing (auth, billing, RBAC, multi-tenancy).

## Product Purpose

Solve Seeds Technologies is an On-Demand SaaS Product Studio and engineering platform that designs, builds, and permanently maintains production-grade software platforms for the next generation of India. 

Rather than acting as transactional freelance contractors who ship code and vanish, Solve Seeds Technologies operates as a permanent engineering partner: deploying a pre-engineered enterprise core (authentication, multi-tenancy, billing/UPI, and audit infrastructure) in 21–35 days, followed by continuous post-delivery maintenance where all future customizations, feature additions, and platform scaling are 100% included under a unified SLA.

## Positioning

"We build the next generation of India."

Unlike freelancers who abandon software after initial deployment and charge unpredictable hourly rates for minor adjustments, and unlike traditional dev agencies that bill 150+ hours to reinvent basic plumbing, Solve Seeds Technologies provides:
1. **Pre-Engineered SaaS Core**: Deployed Day 1 with 100% IP and source code ownership.
2. **Continuous Post-Delivery Maintenance**: Dedicated engineering pods providing ongoing platform evolution, where every customization, security upgrade, and scaling adjustment is completely included.
3. **Engineered for Indian Scale**: Deeply integrated with UPI, GSTN, ONDC, and India-scale high-concurrency infrastructure.

## Operating Context

Founders, enterprise CTOs, and institutional builders across India and global markets evaluating a sovereign, production-grade engineering partner capable of delivering enterprise software with Microsoft-grade discipline, Odoo-grade modularity, and Rippling-grade compound storytelling.
