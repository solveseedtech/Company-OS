# Task: Solve Seeds Technologies Platform

## Design Context

### Users
Founders, CTOs, enterprise heads, and institutional builders across India and international markets seeking a sovereign, production-grade engineering partner rather than transactional freelance coders.

### Brand Personality
Authoritative, Sovereign, Architectural.
- **Voice & Tone**: High-conviction engineering institution, deeply serious, national pride, zero gimmicks or AI clichés.
- **Key National Quotation**: *"We build the next generation of India."*
- **3-Word Personality**: Architectural, Sovereign, Enterprise.
- **Core Narrative**: Complete SaaS engineering platform with a pre-engineered enterprise foundation, followed by continuous post-delivery maintenance where every customization is 100% covered.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual Tone**: Editorial corporate engineering ledger (Odoo modular breadth + Microsoft grid discipline + Rippling compound narrative).
- **Primary Canvas**: Warm tactile paper (`#FFFBE9`) paired with high-contrast deep espresso ink (`#1C1917`) and crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) elevated cards.
- **Accent & Footer**: Architectural desert tan (`#CEAB93`) with structured hairline dividers (`#B5927B`).
- **Structural Dividers**: Exposed 1px solid hairline grid (`#E2D5BE`).
- **Anti-References**: Cheap template clichés (purple gradients, bouncy pill badges, floating drop shadows, shopping cart calculators, generic startup blobs).
- **Typography**: Plus Jakarta Sans for tight, authoritative headlines, Instrument Serif italic accents for editorial emphasis, JetBrains Mono for system metrics, architecture specs, and Indian infrastructure tags.

### Design Principles
1. **Corporate Engineering Authority**: Present Solve Seeds Technologies as a serious, institutional technology platform, not a freelance dev shop or commodity agency.
2. **The India Quotation**: Proudly anchor the national vision: *"We build the next generation of India."*
3. **Continuous Maintenance Differentiator**: Highlight our post-delivery engineering program—all feature customizations, ongoing iterations, and scaling are included.
4. **Exposed Hairline Discipline**: 1px solid borders, micro-rounded 0–6px corners, structured bento ledgers.
5. **100% Day 1 IP Ownership**: Clean repositories, automated infrastructure scripts, and complete code handover with zero vendor lock-in.
