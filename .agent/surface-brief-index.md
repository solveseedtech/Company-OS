# Surface Brief: Solve Seeds Technologies Homepage

## Scope and Visitor Mode
- Surface: `index.html` (Homepage `/`)
- Mode: `Persuade`
- Audience: Non-technical founders, seed-stage venture founders, and enterprise engineering leaders looking to ship production-grade custom SaaS in 21–35 days.
- Job: Evaluate Solve Seeds Technologies as an engineering platform, understand the pre-engineered foundation advantage, inspect live sandboxes, test-drive module assembly, and initiate scoping.
- Primary Action: Click "Scope Your SaaS" or interact with the Mini Module Picker to start custom blueprint configuration.
- Real Proof: 4-layer interactive stack card, 4-cell exposed 1px SLA ledger, live interactive sandbox previews, and head-to-head Agency vs. Studio comparison table.
- Constraints: 0px-6px radii, warm paper canvas (`#FFFBE9`), deep espresso typography (`#1C1917`), exposed hairline borders (`#E2D5BE`), no AI-template clichés.

## Direction Contract

### THESIS
Eliminate the agency "vibe-coded" template cliche. Position Solve Seeds Technologies as a disciplined engineering studio by proving authentication, billing, and tenancy are pre-built commodities, leaving 100% of client investment for custom product logic.

### OWN-WORLD
Warm paper canvas (`#FFFBE9`) structured by 1px solid exposed hairline grids (`#E2D5BE`). Crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) elevated blueprint cards with 3px micro-corners. Deep espresso ink (`#1C1917`) paired with muted umber (`#57504A`) and technical mono tags (`#785A46`). Architectural `#CEAB93` footer with structured ledger dividers.

### STORY
The founder enters, immediately grasps the split architectural hero and 4-layer SaaS stack, verifies the 21–35 day SLA in the 4-cell ledger, clicks modular blocks in the Odoo-style matrix, compares the transparent economics against traditional agencies, and configures their scope.

### FIRST VIEWPORT
64px sticky navbar with geometric logo, venture studio badge, and primary CTA. 55/45 split hero: left side features monospace tag `[ ON-DEMAND SAAS ENGINEERING ]`, tight-tracked headline with italicized editorial touch, value proposition, and dual CTAs; right side features an interactive 4-layer system architecture card with tabbed layer switcher and live technical metrics.

### FORM
Architectural Editorial Ledger (inspired by Microsoft grid discipline, Rippling compound-product cards, and Odoo modular blocks). Seed key: `solve-seeds-arch-ledger-01`.
